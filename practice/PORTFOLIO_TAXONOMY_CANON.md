# Canonical Portfolio Taxonomy

The live Navigator, Pond, Bubbles, Sid and SolCat prototypes use the uploaded source pack as the current portfolio model.

**Portfolio → Practice → Capability → Offering**

## Definitions

- **Portfolio** — What Data#3 does — technology products and services.
- **Practice** — Market-aligned technology domains.
- **Capability** — Key functional areas within a practice.
- **Offering** — What customers buy; includes vendor technology, Data#3 people and process, and one-off or recurring revenue.

## Taxonomy level colours

The colour communicates hierarchy, not technology domain:

- Portfolio: `#11B8F5`
- Practice: `#7F5CE4`
- Capability: `#A20F9F`
- Offering: `#D51BD3`

## Notes

- Capability names are the functional areas from the source pack, for example `Modern Endpoint Management`, `Identity & Access Security` and `Azure & Public Cloud`.
- `Consulting`, `Packaged`, `Project` and `Managed` are treated as commercial motion / offering-type cues where useful; they are not the source Capability layer.
- Source offering names such as `Bespoke Service - Consulting` remain unchanged because they are Salesforce product names, not taxonomy labels.
