Clear accountability

One accountable owner per capability, named by the Practice Manager. Every capability gets the designated point of contact.

02

Index, don't rebuild

It indexes what already exists — nothing migrates and nothing is rebuilt. This is a governance decision, not a platform build.

03

A light, honest cadence

The capability owner is responsible for all offers in their capability area — kept honest by a light cadence: quarterly matrix, monthly record, on-change evidence.
