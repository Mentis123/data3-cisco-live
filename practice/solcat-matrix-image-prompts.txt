SOLCAT MATRIX — CHARACTER AND LOGO IMAGE PROMPTS
Companion to staffnet-prism-image-prompts.txt. Data#3 brand palette only:
Blue Black #000025 · Data#3 Blue #007BC3 · Light Blue #00AEFF · Pale Blue #78DCFF
Aqua #00FFFF · Cool Lilac #9B9BFF · Magenta #FF00FF · Light Grey #9D9FA2

The character is called SOL. Sid the bilby fronts the StaffNet Intelligent
Directory; Sol the cat fronts the SolCat Matrix. They must read as siblings:
same illustration language, same line weight, same flat cel shading.


1) sol-cat.png — the character, primary avatar
Create a friendly cartoon character portrait of a sleek grey Australian
domestic cat, shown head and upper shoulders, facing the viewer, on a fully
transparent background. Flat cel-shaded vector illustration with clean bold
dark navy outlines, soft rounded shapes and warm approachable expression —
the same illustration language as a bespectacled cartoon bilby mascot, so the
two read as a matched pair. The cat wears small round wire-framed dark
sunglasses and a dark navy collared jacket with a subtle upright collar, with
a small circular aqua badge on the chest. Confident half smile, one ear
slightly tilted, alert knowing eyes visible behind the lenses. Colour palette
strictly deep navy #000025, light blue #00AEFF, aqua #00FFFF, pale blue
#78DCFF and neutral greys. Corporate-friendly and charming, not edgy, not
photorealistic, no weapons, no trench coat, no readable text, no logos, no
background elements, no drop shadow.

2) sol-cat-matrix.png — the character in context, for a hero or splash
Same grey cartoon cat character with small round dark glasses and dark navy
jacket, seated calmly at three-quarter view in front of a large softly glowing
grid of translucent cards arranged in rows and columns, suggesting a catalogue
being read at a glance. Thin vertical streams of faint aqua and light blue
particles fall gently behind the grid, abstract and elegant rather than
cinematic. Deep navy #000025 environment, light blue and aqua accents, one
restrained lilac highlight. Flat vector illustration with subtle depth, calm
and premium, corporate-friendly. No readable text, no code glyphs, no logos,
no weapons, no rain-soaked city, no dystopian mood, no empty space.

3) solcat-matrix-mark.png — abstract mark, no character
Create a simple square app-style mark for a solution catalogue. Show a clean
four-by-four geometric grid where one column is illuminated and the others
recede, expressing a catalogue read through a single lens. Each illuminated
cell carries a different subtle pattern — fine dots, diagonal hatch, layered
bars, concentric rings — echoing a practice identity system. Deep navy
#000025 ground, light blue #00AEFF and aqua #00FFFF illumination, restrained
lilac #9B9BFF accent on one cell. Flat, geometric, precise, no gradients
beyond a soft inner glow, no readable text, no letterforms, no logos, no
perspective, no empty space.

4) solcat-matrix-hero.png — optional 16:9 banner
Create a premium 16:9 abstract banner for an internal solution catalogue.
Show a wide matrix of translucent cards in ordered rows and columns, some
cells lit and some deliberately dark and empty, expressing a portfolio where
the gaps matter as much as the coverage. Four vertical bands of light cross
the grid in light blue, aqua, cool lilac and magenta, representing four
commercial motions. Deep navy corporate foundation, elegant restrained depth,
no readable text, no logos, no people, no rainbow treatment, no excessive
glow, no empty space.

NOTES
- Prompt 1 is the one that matters. Generate it first, save beside the HTML as
  sol-cat.png, and embed it as a data URI so the file stays self-contained.
- Keep Sol's glasses small and round. Large mirrored shades read as a costume
  rather than a character, and stop it matching Sid.
- Avoid anything that literally quotes a film: no long black coat, no falling
  green characters, no bullet-time poses. The joke should land through
  posture and the glasses alone, and it keeps the asset safely ours.
- Green is not in the Data#3 palette. The falling-particle motif must be aqua
  and light blue, which is also why prompt 2 says particles rather than code.
