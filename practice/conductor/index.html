One agent, two movements

It keeps work moving through and around the gates.

01

Through the gates

Promotion keeper

Drives stages 5–7 · Experiment to Deploy

  • Watches every case’s stage, open conditions, and next exit decision.
  • Runs the evidence loop across tenant signals, self-audits, and adversarial probes.
  • Assembles gate packs and cues decision-makers. The decision stays human.
02

Around the gates

Certification registrar

Feeds stages 6–10 · ISO 56001 and ISO 42001 trajectory

  • Keeps the documentation spine: profiles, risk records, and gate decisions.
  • Tracks every condition to closure with a named owner and date.
  • Builds certification evidence as a by-product of normal flow.

The tempo of the engine

Gates do not fire on their own.

A stalled portfolio and a moving one differ in cadence. The Conductor keeps every case in motion towards its next gate.

Baton, never rubber stamp. The Conductor prepares and cues every decision; people make them. A gate without a named human decision-maker does not pass.
