🧠 Executive Summary: Building a World-Class AI Practice at Data#3
Data#3 is uniquely positioned to lead the Australian and New Zealand enterprise AI market by launching a vertically integrated, functionally complete AI Practice — anchored by Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) and accelerated by internal IP such as AgentSmith. This proposal outlines a clear, milestone-based strategy that blends thought leadership with execution readiness.
The practice will be built in three distinct phases:
Phase 1: PROVE
Establish a credible, minimal viable practice with lighthouse customers, a functioning FDE model, and initial revenue. Demonstrate real customer value and validate the economics.
Phase 2: VALIDATE
Scale delivery capacity, build repeatable GTM and enablement engines, and prove that the model works across multiple verticals. Activate Microsoft co-sell and build market recognition.
Phase 3: SCALE
Achieve market leadership in ANZ, expand into verticalised offerings and managed services, and build a self-sustaining ecosystem. The practice becomes a strategic growth engine for Data#3.
This framework is not theoretical — it is grounded in proven deployment models, market data, and operational design. It synthesises the best of four leading AI models and translates them into a coherent, actionable roadmap tailored to Data#3’s strengths and ambitions.

🧱 Foundational Recommendations (Cross-Model Consensus)
Across all four models, the following strategic pillars emerged as core building blocks:
1. Vertical Practice Structure

A top-to-bottom organisational model with clear functional layers: leadership, strategy, product, sales, delivery, enablement.
Org-agnostic: roles are defined by function, not existing org chart.

2. Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) as Core Delivery Model

Embedded engineers working inside customer environments.
Weekly delivery of production-grade agents and automations.
Inspired by Palantir, OpenAI, and Anthropic models.

3. Milestone-Based Phasing

Three phases (PROVE, VALIDATE, SCALE) triggered by outcomes, not calendar dates.
Each phase has clear gate criteria: headcount, revenue, customer success, delivery metrics.

4. Internal IP Acceleration

Use of internal accelerators (e.g. AgentSmith) to multiply delivery velocity.
FDEs leverage reusable templates, patterns, and governance kits.

5. Governance by Design

Strong emphasis on responsible AI, security, and compliance.
Governance frameworks span platform, data, and agent layers.

6. Partner-Aligned GTM

Deep integration with Microsoft, Cisco, Dell, HP.
Co-sell, joint marketing, and technical alignment.

🔗 Combined Integrated Strategy
Phase 1: PROVE

Assemble a lean, cross-functional team (15–25 people).
Deliver 3–5 lighthouse customer engagements with embedded FDEs.
Launch AgentSmith v1 and governance starter kits.
Validate economics: >50% margin, <3 weeks to first agent, >65% FDE utilisation.
Exit criteria: 3 referenceable customers, 5 active engagements, stable platform.

Phase 2: VALIDATE

Scale to 45–60 people across all tiers.
Run 15+ FDE engagements with 2–3 pods.
Activate Microsoft co-sell and publish joint case studies.
Launch verticalised offerings and FDE-as-a-Service subscription model.
Exit criteria: 10 reference customers, >30% expansion rate, $5–7M run rate.

Phase 3: SCALE

Expand to 100–135 people, with vertical specialisation and managed services.
FDE becomes a guild with shared standards and continuous improvement.
AgentSmith evolves into a platform with internal and external usage.
Ecosystem motion with partners, ISVs, and Data#3 IP.
Outcome: Data#3 becomes the default choice for enterprise AI in ANZ.


⚠️ Risks, Open Questions, and Opportunities
Risks

Talent pipeline: difficulty hiring and onboarding quality FDEs.
Over-scaling before economics are validated.
Platform over-engineering without real-world feedback.
Market confusion if GTM is not clearly differentiated.

Open Questions

Where should each function live within the existing org (IPT, shared services, etc.)?
How do we balance internal IP with customer ownership and transparency?
What is the optimal pricing model for FDE retainers and accelerators?

Opportunities

Acquisition of boutique AI firms to accelerate growth.
Geographic expansion into Singapore and broader APAC.
Spin-out of AgentSmith as a licensable product.
Analyst positioning as “Palantir of ANZ”.
