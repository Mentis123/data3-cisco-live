# 1. Executive Summary

Data#3 has a clear opportunity to build a differentiated, high-margin AI practice anchored on three pillars:

1. **A vertically integrated practice structure** from strategy → build → run, with clear accountability across leadership, GTM, advisory, delivery, and enablement.  
2. **Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE)** as the core delivery model: small, embedded pods shipping weekly increments of production-ready AI agents and workflows.  
3. **Governed, Microsoft-aligned AI platforms** (M365 Copilot, Azure AI Foundry, Purview, Power Platform) wrapped in Data#3 IP (e.g. AgentSmith, governance blueprints, reference architectures).

Across four independent analyses, there is strong consensus on both *what* needs to exist and *how* it should mature through **three milestone-based phases**:

- **Phase 1 – Foundation & Lighthouse Wins (“Prove the Core”)**  
  Establish a credible AI practice with a minimal but complete structure: named leadership, a tight services catalog (readiness, envisioning, pilot/PoC, initial FDE offer), one prototype FDE pod, and first lighthouse customers. By the end of this phase, AI is no longer a slide—Data#3 has real customers, real agents in production/pilot, and a working governance model.

- **Phase 2 – Scale & Systematise (“Run Multiple Pods Reliably”)**  
  Expand to multiple FDE pods, deepen the bench across advisory, engineering, and OCM, and standardise IP, playbooks, and accelerators. AI becomes a repeatable engine: portfolio is broader, vertical playbooks emerge, AgentSmith and other accelerators are v2 and widely used, and pipeline/revenue are material and trackable.

- **Phase 3 – Platform & Ecosystem (“AI as a Strategic Growth Engine”)**  
  Evolve from “practice” to “platform and ecosystem”: verticalised offerings, managed AI services, tightly integrated partner plays, and FDE embedded into long-term managed contracts. Data#3 is recognised for governed, production-grade AI; FDE is synonymous with how Data#3 delivers AI outcomes.

The combined proposal is **actionable** (clear functions, roles, and phases), **strategic** (governed, partner-aligned, IP-centric), and **execution-ready** (explicit gates, metrics, and a forward-deployed model that can start small and scale).

# 2. Foundational Recommendations (Cross-Model Consensus)

These are the areas where all or most of the models converge. They form the **non-negotiable core** of the AI practice.

## 2.1 Practice Structure & Governance

- **Dedicated AI leadership**  
  - A **Head of AI / Practice Director** accountable for AI strategy, P&L, and partner relationships.  
  - An **AI Practice Manager** overseeing operations, utilisation, staffing, and delivery health.

- **Vertical, function-first structure**  
  Clear separation (but tight collaboration) between:
  - Leadership & Governance  
  - Strategy & Product  
  - Go-to-Market (GTM) & Sales  
  - Consulting & Delivery  
  - FDE organisation  
  - Enablement / IP / Operations (often via IPT)

- **Strong governance as a differentiator**  
  - Governance frameworks spanning Platform, Data, and Agent layers.  
  - Alignment with security, risk, and compliance requirements from day one.  
  - Responsible/ethical AI considerations baked into offers and execution.

## 2.2 Services Portfolio & Customer Lifecycle

- **Lifecycle-aligned portfolio**  
  All models converge on structuring offers around a lifecycle:

  1. **Envision & Readiness** – strategy, readiness assessments, use-case discovery, data and governance baselining.  
  2. **Prototype & Pilot** – Copilot/Foundry/Studio prototypes, structured pilots, and proof-of-value engagements.  
  3. **Production Build** – hardened agents, integration, CI/CD, observability, and performance.  
  4. **Adopt & Scale** – OCM, training, telemetry-driven adoption programs.  
  5. **Operate & Optimise** – ongoing improvement sprints, governance checks, and managed outcomes.

- **Microsoft-centric but platform-aware**  
  - Core stack: M365 Copilot, Copilot Studio, Azure AI Foundry, Azure OpenAI, Power Platform, Entra, Purview.  
  - Complementary ecosystem (Cisco, Dell, HP etc.) recognised but secondary to the Microsoft AI cloud.

## 2.3 Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE)

- **FDE as central delivery model, not a side experiment**  
  - Small, high-calibre pods embedded with customers.  
  - Weekly increments of production-grade agents and workflows.  
  - Continuity from envisioning through build to run.

- **Pods as the atomic unit of execution**  
  Typical consensus pattern:
  - Pod Lead / FDE Principal  
  - 1–3 FDE engineers  
  - Access to shared Solution Architect, OCM, and BA/UX roles.

## 2.4 IP, Accelerators, and IPT

- **Internal accelerators as leverage**  
  - A named accelerator platform (e.g. **AgentSmith**) to boost FDE and delivery productivity.  
  - Governance kits, reference architectures, reusable connectors, and templates.

- **Close partnership with IPT**  
  - IPT captures patterns from FDE and delivery work and converts them into reusable IP.  
  - IPT underpins tooling, experimentation, and internal enablement.

## 2.5 Milestone-Based Growth in Three Phases

- **Three-phase growth model** is consistent across responses:  
  - Phase 1 – prove the model and secure lighthouse wins.  
  - Phase 2 – scale headcount, pods, and portfolio while systematising delivery.  
  - Phase 3 – verticalise, productise, and move into platform/managed services.

- **Gated progression** based on outcomes (customers, revenue, utilisation, IP usage, case studies), not on calendar time.

# 3. Distinctive or Innovative Ideas (Model-Specific Highlights)

These ideas appear in one or two responses but add meaningful strategic advantage.

## 3.1 Thought Leadership & Market Positioning

- **“AI Pulse” / recurring market intelligence report**  
  - Regular publication that benchmarks AI adoption, use cases, and value in ANZ.  
  - Positions Data#3 as a *voice of record* on enterprise AI trends.

- **“AI Forge / AI Studio Labs” workshop series**  
  - Branded, repeatable discovery and prototyping labs for customers.  
  - Acts as a top-of-funnel engine for FDE and pilot engagements.

## 3.2 FDE Evolution & Guild Model

- **FDE “guild” concept**  
  - Cross-practice community with shared standards, playbooks, and career paths.  
  - Keeps FDE quality high and avoids fragmentation as the practice scales.

- **FDE-first internal automation**  
  - Use FDE pods to automate Data#3’s own operations first (e.g., internal support, bid automation).  
  - Generates internal case studies, reduces cost-to-serve, and proves the model before broad external rollout.

## 3.3 Advanced IP & Tooling

- **Multi-agent AI “sidekick” tooling for FDEs**  
  - AI assistants for FDE pods that automate coding, testing, integration wiring, and telemetry setup.  
  - Dramatically increases throughput and consistency.

- **AgentSmith as a named internal platform**  
  - Reusable scaffolding for agents, RAG, evaluation, and governance.  
  - Versioned roadmap (v1, v1.5, v2) aligned with practice phases.

## 3.4 Financials, Metrics, and Gate Reviews

- **Explicit gate criteria for each phase**  
  - Targets around utilisation, revenue/bookings, number of active agents in production, IP reuse rate, and customer satisfaction.  
  - A formal **gate review board** to decide when to hire, invest, or pivot.

- **IP revenue as a tracked metric**  
  - Targets for percentage of AI revenue derived from IP/accelerators (not just time and materials).  
  - Reinforces the strategy of becoming a platform, not just a body-shop.

## 3.5 Cross-Practice and Partner Integration

- **Formal “Practice Council” construct**  
  - Coordination body across AI, Security, Modern Work, Data & Apps, and Infrastructure.  
  - Ensures AI practice amplifies (not competes with) existing practices.

- **Deep co-sell alignment with Microsoft & key OEMs**  
  - Co-branded offers, joint reference architectures, and co-funded pilots.  
  - AI practice positioned as the “brain” on top of existing Data#3 technology strengths.

# 4. Combined Integrated Strategy

This section blends cross-model consensus with the best unique ideas into a single, coherent strategy.

## 4.1 Strategic Positioning

Data#3’s AI practice positions itself as:

> **“The leading ANZ partner for governed, production-grade AI — delivered through embedded Forward Deployed Engineering and powered by Microsoft’s AI cloud and Data#3’s own accelerators.”**

Key differentiators:

- FDE at the core of delivery.  
- Governance and risk as design principles, not afterthoughts.  
- Deep Microsoft alignment plus selective ecosystem partners.  
- IP-heavy execution via AgentSmith and related accelerators.  
- Milestone-gated growth with clear metrics.

## 4.2 Phase 1 – Foundation & Lighthouse Wins

**Objective:** Stand up a credible AI practice, prove the FDE model, and secure lighthouse customers.

**What exists at end of Phase 1:**

- **Leadership & Governance**
  - Head of AI / Practice Director, AI Practice Manager, Governance & Risk Lead.  
  - D3 Governance Framework (Platform/Data/Agent) documented and applied in at least one live customer.

- **Services & GTM**
  - Tight catalog of initial offers:
    - GenAI / Copilot Readiness Assessment  
    - AI Envisioning & Use-Case Workshops (including “AI Forge Labs”)  
    - Pilot/PoC offers for Copilot, Foundry, and Studio  
    - FDE Lite engagement (1 pod, 1 day/week)  
  - Basic sales assets and an initial “AI Pulse” thought-leadership piece.

- **Delivery & FDE**
  - One **prototype FDE pod** (Pod Lead + 1–2 FDEs) plus shared architecture and OCM support.  
  - A small, multi-disciplinary delivery squad covering governance, engineering, and integration.  
  - At least 1–2 lighthouse customers with:
    - Agents in pilot/production  
    - Measured value (e.g., cycle time reduction, ticket deflection, productivity uplift).

- **IPT & IP**
  - AgentSmith v1 and governance starter kits being used on real work.  
  - First internal automation use case (Data#3’s own operations) implemented via FDE.

## 4.3 Phase 2 – Scale & Systematise

**Objective:** Transition from hero projects to a repeatable engine, with multiple pods and a richer portfolio.

**What exists at end of Phase 2:**

- **Leadership & Governance**
  - Matured operating rhythm: capacity planning, gate reviews, and risk management.  
  - Governance patterns reusable across multiple customers and sectors.

- **Services & GTM**
  - Expanded services catalog including:
    - Verticalised offerings (e.g., Public Sector, Health, Education, Commercial scenarios)  
    - FDE as a Service (subscription/retainer model)  
    - Initial managed AI services (operate & optimise).  
  - AI embedded into account planning, with co-sell motions established with Microsoft.

- **Delivery & FDE**
  - 2–3 FDE pods running concurrently, anchored by a **Head of FDE**.  
  - FDE guild established with shared standards, playbooks, and mentoring.  
  - Deeper delivery bench across advisory, AI engineering, data, platform, and OCM.  
  - Engagement Managers and an emerging Customer Success function for AI.

- **IPT & IP**
  - AgentSmith v1.5/v2 and accelerators widely adopted across projects.  
  - IP reuse is normalised; internal tooling and sidekick concepts used by FDE pods.  
  - Regular “AI Pulse” reports and case studies feeding GTM.

## 4.4 Phase 3 – Platform & Ecosystem

**Objective:** Evolve into a platform-driven, vertically specialised AI business with strong partner and IP leverage.

**What exists at end of Phase 3:**

- **Leadership & Governance**
  - AI recognised as a strategic growth engine; metrics tracked at board level.  
  - Governance extended to co-innovation and joint IP with customers and partners.

- **Services & GTM**
  - Vertically structured portfolio with:
    - AI for Public Sector, Health, Education, etc.  
    - Managed AI Agent services and Governed AI Platform offerings.  
  - Ecosystem plays with partners and ISVs, including co-branded solutions.

- **Delivery & FDE**
  - Multiple specialised FDE pods aligned to key verticals and solution domains.  
  - FDE embedded into managed services with continuous improvement sprints contractually baked in.  
  - Robust Customer Success and run-and-optimise capability.

- **IPT & IP**
  - AgentSmith and related accelerators treated as a platform, possibly licensed or co-developed with partners.  
  - Standard experimentation and evaluation framework for agent performance and ROI.

# 5. Risks, Open Questions, and Opportunities

## 5.1 Key Risks

- **Talent scarcity and dilution risk**
  - FDE demands a rare blend of engineering, consulting, and product thinking.  
  - Risk of over-hiring generalists or under-hiring senior anchors.

- **Over-building ahead of demand**
  - Building too many roles/pods/IP before pipeline and revenue justify it.  
  - All models emphasise gates to mitigate this.

- **Internal friction with existing practices**
  - AI practice might be perceived as competing with Modern Work, Data & Apps, Security, etc.  
  - Requires clear role definition and a strong Practice Council.

- **Governance & regulatory complexity**
  - Rapidly evolving responsible AI and data protection expectations.  
  - Missteps could damage trust with customers and partners.

- **Vendor/platform dependency**
  - Heavy alignment with Microsoft is strategic but creates dependency on their roadmap and priorities.

## 5.2 Open Questions

- **Org placement of FDE and IPT alignment**
  - Exact reporting lines: does FDE sit fully inside AI, or partially within delivery/consulting?  
  - How to balance IPT ownership of IP with practice-level priorities?

- **Degree of vertical specialisation vs. horizontal scale**
  - How quickly to verticalise (e.g., Public Sector/Health) vs. staying horizontal with modular playbooks?

- **Commercial models for FDE and IP**
  - How to structure pricing for FDE retainers and IP-heavy offerings.  
  - How to attribute and recognise IP revenue internally.

## 5.3 Opportunities

- **ANZ first-mover advantage on FDE**  
  - Few local partners will offer a Palantir-style FDE delivery model at scale.

- **Internal transformation as proof point**  
  - Using AI and FDE to transform Data#3’s own operations creates powerful case studies and savings.

- **Multi-partner leverage**  
  - Microsoft co-sell, plus hardware/network partners, gives a strong story from “tin” to “agent”.

- **IP-led margins**  
  - AgentSmith and related accelerators create defensible margin and stickiness if executed well.

# 6. Recommended Next Actions / Roadmap

These actions are framed against the three phases and keep the focus on *what* needs to exist.

## 6.1 Immediate Actions (Phase 1 – Next 3–6 Months)

**Leadership & Governance**

- Confirm and appoint:
  - Head of AI / Practice Director  
  - AI Practice Manager  
  - Governance & Risk Lead (with explicit AI remit).
- Finalise and publish the **D3 Governance Framework** (Platform/Data/Agent + pillars) as an internal standard.

**Services & GTM**

- Lock in a **v1 services catalog**:
  - Readiness Assessments  
  - Envisioning & AI Forge Labs  
  - Copilot/Foundry/Studio pilots  
  - FDE Lite engagements.
- Produce core GTM assets:
  - One slide “AI Practice on a Page” for leadership and partners.  
  - Initial “AI Pulse” insight summary.

**Delivery & FDE**

- Stand up the **prototype FDE pod**:
  - Nominate Pod Lead / FDE Principal.  
  - Assign 1–2 FDE engineers.  
- Select 1–2 **internal** use cases and 1–2 **lighthouse customers** to prove the model.  
- Define a simple FDE weekly cadence (backlog, build, demo, deploy, measure).

**IPT & IP**

- Formalise collaboration with IPT:
  - Scope and stand up AgentSmith v1 (minimum capabilities for scaffolding agents and enforcing governance patterns).  
- Start capturing patterns from the first pilots and FDE work as reusable modules.

**Gating Prep**

- Define **Phase 1 exit criteria**, such as:
  - Number of successful pilots/lighthouse customers.  
  - At least one internal and one external case study.  
  - FDE pod running at sustainable cadence.

---

## 6.2 Medium-Term Actions (Phase 2 – Following 6–12+ Months)

**Leadership & Governance**

- Establish a **gate review board** to decide on scaling hires and investments.  
- Evolve risk and governance standards based on Phase 1 learning.

**Services & GTM**

- Expand the services catalog with:
  - Verticalised offerings for 2–3 priority industries.  
  - FDE as a Service (subscription) and initial managed AI offers.  
- Embed AI practice into **strategic account planning** and formalise co-sell motions with Microsoft.

**Delivery & FDE**

- Grow to **2–3 FDE pods**, ensuring:
  - FDE guild standards and training are in place.  
  - Engagement Managers can coordinate larger, multi-pod programs.  
- Build out bench depth in governance consulting, AI engineering, integration, and OCM.

**IPT & IP**

- Advance AgentSmith and related accelerators to **v1.5/v2**, including:
  - Evaluation harnesses  
  - Observability patterns  
  - Additional prebuilt integrations.  
- Make IP reuse a standard expectation on all AI engagements.

**Gating Prep**

- Agree Phase 2 gate criteria:
  - Minimum number of active FDE engagements.  
  - Revenue and utilisation thresholds.  
  - IP reuse percentages.  
  - Customer satisfaction and case study volume.

---

## 6.3 Longer-Term Actions (Phase 3 – Platform & Ecosystem)

**Leadership & Governance**

- Position the AI practice as a **core business pillar** with board-level visibility.  
- Evolve governance to support joint innovation and IP with customers and partners.

**Services & GTM**

- Launch **managed AI agent** and **governed AI platform** offerings.  
- Formalise and market **vertical portfolios** (e.g., Public Sector AI, Health AI) with dedicated GTM plays.

**Delivery & FDE**

- Align FDE pods by vertical and solution domain.  
- Fully integrate FDE into managed service contracts with continuous improvement obligations.

**IPT & IP**

- Treat AgentSmith and associated accelerators as a **platform**:
  - Potential external monetisation and co-development with key partners.  
- Implement standard experimentation and evaluation frameworks for all agents in production.

---

This roadmap keeps Data#3 focused on the **right “what” at each phase**: starting with a credible core, proving FDE, and then deliberately scaling into a platform and ecosystem model that compounds value over time.
